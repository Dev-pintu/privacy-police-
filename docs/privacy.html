Privacy Policy

PM Academy · Effective date: 4 November 2026

Summary: PM Academy collects only what the app needs to work — your account details, your study progress and the content you submit. We do not sell your data, do not show ads, and do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

PM Academy is a study application for school students. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. For privacy questions contact academypm331@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

a) Account information

  • Email address and a hashed password (if you sign up with email), or your Google account's name and email (if you sign up with Google Sign-In).
  • Your display name, class/grade and an optional profile photo.
  • Parental-consent record: whether a parent/guardian consented, when, and (optionally) the parent's email address — required by India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act for child users.

b) Study data

  • Your progress (topics viewed, papers attempted, revision marks).
  • Group-study activity you create: room names, whiteboard strokes and chat-style operations while you are in a room.
  • Which in-app notifications you have read.

c) Information you submit

  • Feedback form entries: name, class, email, feedback type and description (feedback may be sent signed-out; then only the details you type are stored).
  • Search queries, used internally to improve content discoverability.

d) Information stored only on your device

  • Preferences such as selected class, theme, board colours and recent searches. These stay on the device and are cleared when you clear app data or uninstall.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To sync your progress and profile across devices.
  • To provide group-study features with other room members.
  • To respond to feedback and support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations, including parental-consent requirements for child users.

4. Service providers

Data is processed by the infrastructure the app runs on:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage and app functions.
  • Google — Google Sign-In (only your name and email are shared with us via sign-in; Google's own privacy policy applies to Google).
  • App stores (Google Play, Uptodown) — governed by their own policies.

We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone. We do not embed advertising or marketing-tracking SDKs.

5. Sharing

  • Other students: in a group-study room, your display name and board contributions are visible to room members.
  • Administrators: app administrators can see feedback and content-related data to operate the service.
  • Legal: if required by law, court order or to protect rights and safety.

6. Data retention & deletion

  • Data is kept while your account exists.
  • Delete everything: Profile → Delete Account removes your account, profile, photo, progress and associated records permanently. Feedback you already submitted may be retained anonymised for service improvement.
  • Local preferences are removed when you clear app data or uninstall.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), passwords are stored hashed by the authentication provider, and database access is restricted by row-level security so each account only reaches its own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit collection to what the app needs.

8. Children's privacy

PM Academy is designed for school students and requires verifiable parent or guardian consent before an account can be created (India DPDP Act; where COPPA applies, verifiable parental consent is obtained the same way). Parents may request review or deletion of their child's data by emailing academypm331@gmail.com.

9. Your rights

  • Access, correct or update your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account and data from Profile → Delete Account.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask questions, by emailing academypm331@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when practices change and revise the effective date above. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Email: academypm331@gmail.com
